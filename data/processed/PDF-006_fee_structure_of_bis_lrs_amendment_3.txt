2. Clause 6.4.1 to be amended as below: 

The laboratory applying for inclusion has to submit undertaking given in 
Annex A declaring that the test facilities applied for are actually available in the 
laboratory. In case the application is not fit to be processed ahead, the 
application is to be clos ed and reason for closure informed to the laboratory. 
The laboratory can re -apply for inclusion after taking corrective actions. The 
verification of the IS included in the scope of recognition will be carried out 
through verification audit. 
In case the tes t facilities for the products applied for are not found available in 
the laboratory during the verification audit, the IS included will be deleted from 
the scope of the laboratory with immediate effect and the laboratory will not be 
able to apply for inclu sion for a cooling off period of three months form the 
date of deletion of IS from its scope. 
In case of any other major deviations found during the verification a udit, action 
as per BIS LRS can be taken against the laboratory.
